Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-08-24 for the in-game click-to-move marker.

## `cinnabar_ruyi_arrow.png`

- Mode: new transparent raster asset (`stylized-concept`).
- Prompt intent: a compact, unmistakable downward arrow with a muted cinnabar lacquer center, thin weathered brass rim, two small inward ruyi-cloud notches and one dark-jade square inset. The silhouette remains a simple standard arrow at 40–55 px, with no needle, talisman, tassel or excessive ornament, isolated on true transparency.

## `geomantic_floor_seal.png`

- Mode: new raster asset followed by targeted `background-extraction` in the built-in image generation workflow.
- Prompt intent: a genuinely flat, wide floor-perspective geomantic tracing made only from soot, ochre powder, faded cinnabar brush marks and minute verdigris dust. The 3:1 shallow ellipse is baked into the art itself; it has no metal thickness, bevels, bolts, highlights, cast shadow or independent glow. The final extracted PNG uses true transparency.

Both source PNGs retain alpha transparency. They are runtime-scaled by Phaser; the original high-resolution files are kept here as the editable master assets.
