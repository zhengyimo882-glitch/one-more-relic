# Antique Shop Top-down Background v1

- File: `shop_topdown_background.png`
- Purpose: strict orthographic 2D antique-shop environment background
- Generated with: built-in ImageGen tool
- Reference: `见好不收UI风格板.png` (palette and material language only)
- Integration size: rendered at `1280 × 720` by Phaser from the generated `1672 × 941` source

The prompt required a fixed overhead camera, no isometric/2.5D perspective,
clear furniture footprints, a south entrance, north service counter, central
cleaning workbench, west research/pledge zone, east display/sales zone and a
northeast atlas desk. No UI or text is baked into the image.

## Display cabinet and cloth

- `display_cabinet.png`: generated as an isolated ornate walnut, brass and
  dark-jade glass cabinet for the growth reveal.
- `display_cloth.png`: generated as the matching tied charcoal canvas cover.
- Both used a flat green chroma background in built-in ImageGen, followed by
  local chroma removal with soft matte, despill and a one-pixel edge contract.
